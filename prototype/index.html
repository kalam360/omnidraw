omnidraw

A teacher's cockpit for live YouTube classes — branded Excalidraw canvas, agent-driven visualizations, single-purpose tooling. Adapted from linear-app (Apache-2.0). Cyan accent (chalk on blackboard) replaces Linear's violet.
